Chapter I — Who I Am

Curious, Explorer & Kind

✦ Curious ✦ Explorer ✦ Kind

Hi there! If I had to sum up who I am in just three words, they would be Curious, Explorer, and Kind. I love asking "how does this work?" and finding thoughtful, creative answers—whether I am writing lines of code or getting lost in a great story.

In my free time, you will usually find me between two worlds: programming interactive ideas on my laptop or reading novels that spark my imagination. Both remind me that patience, empathy, and curiosity can turn small beginnings into something unforgettable.

Three Words, One Story

  • Curious: Always eager to understand new ideas, logic, and perspectives.
  • Explorer: Excited to try new things—from coding challenges to unfamiliar book genres.
  • Kind: Believing warmth, patience, and genuine care matter in everything I do.
